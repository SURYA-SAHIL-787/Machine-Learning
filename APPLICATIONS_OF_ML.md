# APPLICATIONS OF MACHINE LEARNING

# ============================================================

Machine Learning is used in almost every industry today.
Below are some major real-world applications.

---

1. HEALTHCARE

---

* Disease prediction
* Medical image analysis (X-rays, MRI)
* Drug discovery

Example:
Predicting diabetes using patient data

---

2. FINANCE

---

* Fraud detection
* Credit scoring
* Stock price prediction

Example:
Detecting unusual transactions in bank accounts

---

3. E-COMMERCE

---

* Recommendation systems
* Customer behavior analysis
* Dynamic pricing

Example:
Amazon recommending products

---

4. SOCIAL MEDIA

---

* Face recognition
* Content recommendation
* Spam detection

Example:
Instagram suggesting reels

---

5. TRANSPORTATION

---

* Self-driving cars
* Traffic prediction
* Route optimization

Example:
Google Maps predicting traffic

---

6. EDUCATION

---

* Personalized learning
* Student performance prediction

Example:
Recommending courses based on performance

---

7. ENTERTAINMENT

---

* Movie recommendations
* Music suggestions

Example:
Netflix recommending shows

---

## WHY ML IS IMPORTANT

* Automates decision making
* Improves accuracy
* Saves time and cost
* Handles large-scale data
